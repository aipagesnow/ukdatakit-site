UK public data tools

Clean UK public data, ready to use.

ukdatakit makes two pay-per-result tools on the Apify Store: UK House Prices (sold prices from HM Land Registry) and UK Visa Sponsor Register (the Home Office list of licensed sponsors). They are for analysts, property professionals, recruiters, job seekers and compliance teams. You pay per result on Apify and export to JSON, CSV or Excel.

Our tools

Available on the Apify Store. Run them in your browser, on a schedule, or through the API.

UK House Prices

Source: HM Land Registry Price Paid Data

  • Sold prices by full postcode, sector or outcode
  • Filter by date range
  • Summary statistics including the median price
What you get, one row per sale:
  • Price and sale date
  • Full address and its parts, postcode and outcode
  • Property type, new build, tenure and transaction category
  • Link to the record on HM Land Registry

Plus a run summary including the median price.

Open on Apify

UK Visa Sponsor Register

Source: Home Office register of licensed sponsors

  • Search the licensed sponsor list by organisation name, town and route
  • Check whether an employer can sponsor Skilled Worker visas
  • Change monitor mode: track sponsors added to or removed from the register
  • Useful for recruiters, job seekers and compliance checks
What you get, one row per sponsor:
  • Organisation name, town and county
  • Routes, types and ratings, and overall rating
  • Skilled Worker flag
  • Register date and a link to the GOV.UK CSV
