UK public data tools

Clean UK public data, ready to use.

ukdatakit makes pay-per-result tools on the Apify Store for official UK public data. That includes sold house prices, planning constraints, the visa sponsor register, Companies House accounts and ID checks, food business leads, school jobs, waste carrier licences, nurseries and government consultations. They are for analysts, property professionals, recruiters, accountants, compliance teams, public affairs teams and B2B sales teams. You pay per result on Apify and export to JSON, CSV or Excel.

UK House Prices

Source: HM Land Registry Price Paid Data

  • Sold prices by full postcode, sector or outcode
  • Filter by date range
  • Sale history for one house, with the gain since its previous sale
  • Summary statistics including the median price
What you get, one row per sale:
  • Price and sale date
  • Full address and its parts, postcode and outcode
  • Property type, new build, tenure and transaction category
  • Link to the record on HM Land Registry

Plus a run summary including the median price.

UK Visa Sponsor Register

Source: Home Office register of licensed sponsors

  • Search the licensed sponsor list by organisation name, town and route
  • Paste a list of employers to check which can sponsor Skilled Worker visas
  • Change monitor mode: track sponsors added to or removed from the register
  • Useful for recruiters, job seekers and compliance checks
What you get, one row per sponsor:
  • Organisation name, town and county
  • Routes, types and ratings, and overall rating
  • Skilled Worker flag
  • Register date and a link to the GOV.UK CSV

UK Planning Constraints Checker

Source: planning.data.gov.uk (MHCLG)

  • Check a list of postcodes or coordinates in England in one go
  • Conservation area, listed building, Article 4, tree preservation order, and live Environment Agency flood zones 2 and 3
  • Also green belt, national park, National Landscape, SSSI, scheduled monument and World Heritage Site
  • Tells you when a council hasn't published a layer, so a "no" is never really "unknown"
What you get, one row per location:
  • A yes/no column and name for each constraint
  • Nearby listed buildings with grade and distance
  • A plain-English note on what each constraint means
  • Council name and a link to the official map

Companies House ID Verification Checker

Source: Companies House public data API

  • Check which directors and PSCs have verified their identity under ECCTA
  • Bulk company numbers, with a due date for each person still to verify
  • A plain action summary per company, plus a chase list CSV of everyone still to verify
  • Useful for accountants, company secretaries and compliance teams
What you get, one row per company:
  • Company name, status and number of people in scope
  • Who is verified and who is outstanding
  • Due dates and overdue flags
  • Link to the company on Companies House

UK Food Business Leads

Source: Food Standards Agency hygiene ratings + Companies House

  • Restaurants, cafés, takeaways, pubs and food shops by council or postcode district
  • Spot new openings: businesses awaiting their first hygiene inspection
  • Matched to the limited company behind each premises, with a confidence level
  • Weekly monitor for new listings, rating changes and closures
What you get, one row per food business:
  • Name, type, address, postcode and council
  • Hygiene rating, inspection date and scores
  • Company name, number, type and incorporation date
  • A PECR flag for limited companies, plus links to the FSA and Companies House pages

UK School Jobs & Hiring Schools

Source: Department for Education Teaching Vacancies

  • Every live job on Teaching Vacancies, the DfE’s national job site for schools in England
  • Filter by keyword, region, town, postcode, job category, working pattern, salary and dates
  • Switch the output to one row per school that is hiring, with its open roles
  • Schedule a daily feed of jobs not seen in earlier runs
What you get, one row per job:
  • Job title, category, working pattern and salary
  • School name, URN, website, town, region and postcode
  • Posted and closing dates, and a link to the advert
  • Or one row per hiring school, with its open roles and earliest closing date

UK Company Accounts

Source: Companies House Accounts Data Product

  • Turnover, profit, cash, net assets, equity and average employees from electronically filed accounts
  • Last year’s figures and turnover growth
  • Filter fresh filings by company number, name, turnover, cash, profit or headcount
  • No Companies House API key needed
What you get, one row per company:
  • Company name and number, and the accounts period
  • Turnover, profit, cash, debtors, stock, net assets and employees
  • Previous period figures and turnover growth
  • A link to the filing history on Companies House

UK Waste Carrier Licence Check

Source: Environment Agency public register (England)

  • Check a licence by registration number, business name or postcode
  • Find new registrations from the last N days, or upper-tier licences due to expire
  • Numbers that are not on the register return a clear “not found” row, at no charge
  • Sole traders are left out of lead lists by default
What you get, one row per registration:
  • Registration number, holder name, company number and applicant type
  • Tier, registration type, status, and registration and expiry dates
  • Address, locality and postcode
  • A link to the official register entry

UK Nurseries & Childcare

Source: Ofsted childcare providers and inspections

  • Registered nurseries, pre-schools and out-of-school clubs in England
  • Filter by council, region, town, postcode, setting type, places, rating and dates
  • Find newly opened settings, or filter by the latest Ofsted rating or report-card grades
  • Childminders are redacted by Ofsted and are never returned
What you get, one row per provider:
  • Ofsted URN, name, setting type, address, council and region
  • Registered places
  • Latest Ofsted rating, or the grades from the new report card
  • A link to the Ofsted report
