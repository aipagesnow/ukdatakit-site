UK Waste Carrier Licence Check & New Registrations

Source: Environment Agency public register (England)

Check a waste carrier licence in seconds, or build a list of new and renewing waste businesses. Paste registration numbers (for example CBDU128084), business names or postcodes and you get the official Environment Agency register entry for England: tier, carrier, broker or dealer type, registration and expiry dates, and status.

Open on Apify

Who it’s for

People use it to:

  • Households, builders, landlords and councils check that a carrier is registered, as part of their duty of care when handing over waste.
  • Waste brokers, facilities managers and procurement teams bulk-check suppliers and their expiry dates.
  • B2B sales teams in insurance, vehicle finance, tracking, compliance training and software get new waste businesses each week.
  • Compliance consultants chase licences that are about to expire.

What it does

  • Check mode: one row per match for each registration number, name or postcode. Numbers that are not on the register return a clear “not found” row.
  • New mode (the default): every registration added in the last N days (default 7).
  • Expiring mode: upper-tier licences that run out in the next N days (default 30). Lower-tier registrations never expire.
  • Filter by tier, postcode prefix or town. Sole traders are left out of new and expiring lists by default. Check mode always shows what you asked for.
  • The default input returns new registrations from the last 7 days (up to 100). No API key and no login.

What you get, one row per registration

  • Registration number, holder name, company number and applicant type
  • Tier, registration type, status, and registration and expiry dates
  • Address, locality and postcode
  • A link to the official register entry

Common questions

Is “not found” proof that a carrier is unregistered?

No. It means there is no match on the England register for exactly what you typed. Check the spelling, try the business name, and check the Welsh, Scottish or Northern Irish registers.

Why are sole traders left out?

Lead lists are limited to companies and partnerships by default, to keep personal data to a minimum. Check mode always shows what you asked for.

How often should I run New mode?

Weekly with a 7-day look-back, or daily with a 2-day look-back.
