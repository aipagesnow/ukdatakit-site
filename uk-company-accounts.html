UK Company Accounts – Turnover, Profit & Net Assets

Source: Companies House Accounts Data Product

Get financial figures from UK company accounts: turnover, gross and operating profit, profit after tax, cash, debtors, stock, net assets, equity and average employees. Each figure comes with last year’s value, and turnover growth is included when both years are present.

Open on Apify

Who it’s for

People use it to:

  • B2B sales and lead generation teams filter fresh filers by turnover, cash, profit or headcount, for example companies with turnover over £5m and 20–250 staff that filed accounts this week.
  • Accountants, brokers, lenders and M&A advisers spot growing or cash-rich companies, and new year-ends.
  • Credit and supplier checks pull the latest headline numbers for a list of company numbers.
  • Researchers and journalists analyse company performance across thousands of filings.

What it does

  • Read Companies House’s free Accounts Data Product: the daily bulk files of every set of accounts filed electronically (iXBRL). That’s typically 25,000–40,000 companies a day.
  • Look up specific company numbers, or filter by company name, turnover, net assets, cash, profit and employee counts.
  • Scan the latest daily file, or up to 20 recent files (Companies House keeps the last 60 working days).
  • Tick “Only accounts that show turnover” if you want to skip micro companies that usually file a balance sheet only.
  • The default input reads the latest daily file and returns 50 companies that publish turnover. No Companies House API key is needed, and the actor does not scrape the Companies House website.

What you get, one row per company

  • Company name and number, and the accounts period
  • Turnover, profit, cash, debtors, stock, net assets and employees
  • Previous period figures and turnover growth
  • A link to the filing history on Companies House

Common questions

Does it cover every company?

It covers companies that filed accounts electronically in the daily files you scan. That’s most small and micro companies and many larger ones. Some large companies file on paper or as PDF, and those are not included.

Can I look up any company number?

Only if that company filed accounts in the files you scan. A company files once a year, so scan more days (up to 20) to raise the chance, or use the official Companies House service for older accounts.

Why is turnover missing?

UK micro and small companies can file “filleted” accounts with no profit and loss account. Tick “Only accounts that show turnover” to keep only companies that publish it.

Are directors or personal data included?

No. You get company-level financial figures only.
