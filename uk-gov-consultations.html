UK Government Consultations Tracker

Source: GOV.UK search API

Get UK government consultations and calls for evidence published on GOV.UK: open ones, sorted by closing date, with days left to respond; closed ones still waiting for a government response; and newly published outcomes. Schedule a watchlist so you don’t miss a deadline that affects your sector.

Open on Apify

Who it’s for

People use it to:

  • Trade bodies, charities and campaign groups respond before deadlines on policy that affects their members.
  • Public affairs, law and consultancy firms run client alerts by department or keyword, for example “VAT”, “planning” or “social care”.
  • Businesses find out early about tax, regulation and licensing changes.
  • Journalists and researchers see what government is asking about and when responses come out.

What it does

  • Filter by status (open, closed or outcome), keyword or department (HM Treasury, HMRC, the Department for Education, MHCLG, DESNZ and every other GOV.UK organisation).
  • Limit open consultations to those closing in the next N days, or outcomes published or updated in the last N days.
  • Schedule “Only new since the last run” for alerts. The first run saves a baseline. Use a different watchlist name for each alert.
  • The default input returns all consultations open right now (about 60–80 at a time). No API key and no login.
  • You get the title, summary, dates, departments and a link. Open the link for the full documents and how to respond.

What you get, one row per consultation

  • Title, summary, type and status
  • Opening and closing dates, and days left
  • Department names
  • A link to the GOV.UK page

Status is “open”, “closed – awaiting outcome” or “outcome published”. If GOV.UK still labels a consultation as open after its closing date, the actor reports it as closed.

No personal data. Documents are government publications. The tool includes UK government departments and agencies that publish on GOV.UK. The Scottish and Welsh Governments and the Northern Ireland Executive publish many consultations on their own sites, and those are not included.

Common questions

Does it include devolved governments?

It includes UK government departments and agencies that publish on GOV.UK. The Scottish and Welsh Governments and the Northern Ireland Executive publish many consultations on their own sites, and those aren’t included.

Does it include the full consultation text or response forms?

No. You get the title, summary, dates, departments and link. Open the link for documents and how to respond.

How do I find a department’s slug?

Open its GOV.UK page, for example gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-treasury, and use the last part of the URL (hm-treasury).
